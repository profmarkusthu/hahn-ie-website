## Supervised Doctoral Theses

### Amir Laribi (2024)
*High Resolution Radar based 3D Estimation of Extended Targets for Automotive Applications*

Focus: SAR imaging, height estimation, and 3D reconstruction with automotive radar.

### Ole Schumann (2021)
*Machine Learning Applied to Radar Data: Classification and Semantic Instance Segmentation of Moving Road Users*

Focus: Deep learning for radar point cloud processing and object classification.

### Jakob Lombacher (2021)
*Semantische Umgebungserfassung auf Basis von Radar-Merkmalskarten*

Focus: Temporal representations and localization in dynamic environments.
