Research Focus

My research centers on making sensors and edge devices “think” - bringing AI capabilities directly to the point of data collection:

  • Edge AI: Local processing for privacy and efficiency
  • Radar Scene Understanding: Deep learning for automotive radar
  • Sensor Fusion: Multi-modal perception for autonomous systems
  • Distributed Systems: Software architectures for embedded AI
